🌱 About The Clean Swap Shop

We're on a mission to make clean living easy, affordable, and accessible for every family.

"You shouldn't need a chemistry degree to know what's safe for your family. We make the research easy so you can make the swap."

Our Story

The Clean Swap Shop started with a simple realization: the products most of us grew up using — the name-brand cleaners, the familiar shampoos, the food we stocked our pantries with — were quietly exposing our families to hundreds of synthetic chemicals every single day.

After spending years researching ingredient lists, reading EWG reports, and testing cleaner alternatives, we realized the information was out there — but scattered, confusing, and hard to act on. So we built The Clean Swap Shop: a one-stop resource to make the swap simple.

Every product we recommend has been researched for its ingredients, effectiveness, and value. We tell you exactly what's in the conventional version, why it's a concern, and which natural alternative actually works just as well (or better).

How It Works

It's simple. Browse by category, find a product you use, and click the "Shop the Swap" button. You'll be taken directly to our recommended clean alternative. When you purchase through our links, we may earn a small affiliate commission — at no extra cost to you.

That commission is what keeps this site free, ad-light, and independent. We never recommend a product just because it pays more — only because we genuinely believe it's the best clean option available.
