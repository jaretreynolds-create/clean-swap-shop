What’s actually in chemical sunscreen?

Chemical (also called organic-filter) sunscreens rely on UV-absorbing molecules. Three names show up again and again on labels people are trying to leave behind:

  • Oxybenzone. A common UV filter detected in human biomonitoring studies; also flagged in reef-protection conversations. Many clean and reef-safe lists simply avoid it.
  • Octinoxate (octyl methoxycinnamate). Another absorbing filter often paired with oxybenzone; likewise restricted or discouraged in some reef-protection contexts.
  • Homosalate. A salicylate-type filter used to boost SPF numbers; another ingredient clean-sun seekers typically skip when they move to mineral.

Sun protection still matters — this swap is not “skip SPF.” It is “choose a filter system that sits on skin and keeps the ingredient list short,” especially for faces, kids, and long beach days.

FAQ

Does mineral SPF work as well as chemical?

Broad-spectrum mineral formulas with adequate zinc (and sometimes titanium) protect when applied generously and reapplied. The failure mode is usually under-application — not the mineral itself.

How do I reduce the white cast?

Use enough product but rub thoroughly; warm it in your hands first; consider tinted mineral options for face. A faint cast beats an invisible skim that leaves ears and part-lines burned.

Is Badger reef-safe?

Badger markets mineral, reef-conscious sun care without the chemical filters many reef guidelines flag. Still read the specific product listing — “reef-safe” claims vary by formula and by how destinations define the term.

Are oxybenzone and octinoxate definitely “toxic”?

We stay research-minded, not alarmist. These filters are effective UV absorbers; concerns about systemic absorption, hormones, and reefs are why many families prefer mineral for daily and beach use. This is a caution-based preference swap, not a diagnosis.

Can I use mineral SPF under makeup?

Yes — apply, let it set a minute, then makeup. If pilling happens, use a lighter moisturizer underneath or a face-specific mineral formula.
