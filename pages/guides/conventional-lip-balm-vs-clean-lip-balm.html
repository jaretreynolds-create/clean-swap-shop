Why people move away from conventional lip balm

  • Lips are a shortcut to your mouth. Whatever is in the tube gets tasted. A short ingredient list is an easy upgrade for something you reapply all day.
  • Petrolatum is a barrier, not a nourisher. It seals moisture in, but it isn’t a plant oil. Many people prefer oils and beeswax that soften as they protect.
  • “Flavor” and “fragrance” hide blends. Under U.S. rules, companies can list a scent or flavor blend as one word, so you can’t see what’s inside.

You don’t need to toss the tube in your pocket tonight. Finish it, then let the next one be simpler.

FAQ

Is Badger lip balm really only four ingredients?

Yes, per the current listing: organic extra virgin olive oil, organic beeswax, organic castor oil, and organic rosemary extract. Always double-check the tube you receive.

Why unscented instead of mint or citrus?

Unscented is the gentlest option for sensitive lips and for gifting when you don’t know someone’s flavor preferences. Badger also makes scented Classics if you want variety later.

Does it contain petroleum jelly?

No. Badger markets the Classic line as free of petrolatum. The base is plant oils and beeswax.

Is the 4-pack the right size for gifts?

Yes for stocking stuffers and for keeping one tube in each winter coat. Each tube is 0.15 oz — the classic stick size.

Will it melt in a hot car?

Like most beeswax balms, extreme heat can soften it. Keep it out of a parked car on a hot day when you can.
