Why people move away from synthetic fleece

  • Fleece sheds plastic fibers. Those tiny pieces go down the drain in the wash and into the air as the throw is used. Wool doesn’t shed microplastics.
  • It’s a plastic fabric against your skin for a long evening. A breathable natural fiber stays comfortable when the room is warm and the movie is long.
  • Many fleeces are finished with extra treatments. Stain- or water-resistant coatings can add chemistry you didn’t mean to buy. A simple wool throw keeps the list shorter.

You don’t need to exile the fleece already on the chair. Use it up, then let the next one be wool.

Is the throw really machine washable?

Yes, per the current listing. That’s the point of the Eco-Wise line. Still follow the care label — wool can felt if it’s agitated or dried too hot.
