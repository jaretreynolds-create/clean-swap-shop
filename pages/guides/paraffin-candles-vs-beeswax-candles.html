What’s actually in a typical scented candle?

Candles are not a reason to panic. They are a cozy ritual, and one candle on a Sunday night is not the problem. The clean-living question is about what you’re choosing to burn indoors for hours at a time, especially in fall and winter when the windows stay shut:

  • Paraffin wax. The most common candle wax, refined from petroleum. It is inexpensive and holds scent well. Some households would rather burn a renewable, single-ingredient wax.
  • Synthetic fragrance. “Apple orchard” and “fireside” are usually fragrance blends listed as one word on the label. Fragrance can fill a room fast, which is the point of the product and also why sensitive noses notice.
  • Dyes. Bright orange and deep plum jars are often colored wax. It looks festive but adds one more thing to burn.
  • Soot. Any candle can smoke when the wick is too long or the flame flickers in a draft. That black ring on the jar or the wall is a cue to trim and adjust, whatever the wax.

You don’t need to throw out the candle collection tonight. Burn what you love, then let the next restock be beeswax.

How to switch (step by step)

  1. Burn down what you have (or gift it). No guilt pile. Finish favorites, then stop restocking scented paraffin jars.
  2. Order pure beeswax. Use our tagged Amazon link for the current listing: Shop Big Dipper Pure Beeswax Tealights.
  3. Read the label. Look for “100% pure beeswax.” Phrases like “made with beeswax” or “beeswax blend” can mean mostly another wax.
  4. Trim wicks and skip drafts. Keep wicks short and candles away from fans and open windows for a steadier, cleaner flame with any wax.
  5. Want scent? Keep it light. Try a simmer pot (orange peel, cinnamon stick, cloves) on the stove next to unscented beeswax candles instead of a heavily fragranced jar.
  6. Practice basic candle safety. Use a heat-safe holder, never leave a flame unattended, and keep candles away from kids, pets, and fall décor.

FAQ

Are soy candles a clean swap too?

Soy wax is plant-based and a step away from paraffin, but many soy candles are blends and many are heavily fragranced. If you go soy, look for 100% soy with no added fragrance (or only essential oils). We feature beeswax here because unscented pure beeswax keeps the label shortest.

Do beeswax candles smell like anything?

Unscented pure beeswax has a faint, natural honey note, especially before lighting. It won’t fill a room the way a fragrance-loaded jar does. For fragrance-sensitive homes, that’s the point.

What’s the white coating on my beeswax candles?

That’s called “bloom,” and it’s normal for pure beeswax. Wipe it with a soft cloth or warm it gently with your hands if you want the golden color back.

Do beeswax candles burn longer?

Beeswax is dense and generally burns slowly, so many people find beeswax candles last well. Burn time depends on size, wick, and drafts, so check the listing for the specific product.

Are tealights safe without a cup?

Always burn tealights in a heat-safe holder sized for them. Check whether your pack includes cups and pick holders to match.
