Under-Sink Swap Checklist

Open the cabinet. Soften the chemistry. Keep the calm.

A one-sitting guide for bleach burn, mystery “fresh” scent, dish soap that foams forever, and laundry pods parked next to the trash bags. No shame, no 40-product overhaul — just clearer air and kinder counters.

Print tip: Landscape or portrait both work. Check boxes as you go. Keep this on the fridge or taped inside the cabinet door.

How to use this checklist

  1. Open the under-sink cabinet (and the laundry shelf if detergent lives nearby).
  2. For each item: note the toxic clue, consider the clean swap, shop only if you’re ready.
  3. Finish what you already own when you can — then replace empty bottles with the softer option.
  4. Browse the full library anytime: All Swaps.

Soft counters. Clearer breath. Swap slow.

Toxic clue
Sharp chlorine smell; sodium hypochlorite; “kills 99.9%” marketed for daily counters; never mix with ammonia or acids. Everyday bleach wipe-downs can irritate airways and leave residue where coffee cups and kid snacks land.
Clean swap
Branch Basics Concentrate — plant-based concentrate you dilute for all-purpose kitchen wipe-downs. Same spray-and-wipe ritual, gentler ingredient story. Save harsh bleach for rare, well-ventilated deep cleans if you still need it.
