Overview

The Clean Swap Shop ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We may collect limited information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and message content when you use our contact page or subscribe to our newsletter (when that feature is connected to a backend service).
