Why Make the Swap?

Everyday household products often contain chemicals linked to allergies, hormone disruption, and long-term health risks — and most of us have no idea.

  • The average home contains 62 synthetic chemicals in cleaning products alone
  • Many are linked to asthma, infertility, and cancer with long-term exposure
  • Natural alternatives work just as well — often better
  • Every swap supports sustainable, ethical brands
  • Small changes add up to a healthier home and planet
