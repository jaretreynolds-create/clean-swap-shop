## Day 2 — Omega-6 / oxidized lipids / hexane education

**Hook**  
“Vegetable oil” on the front doesn’t always mean quiet under heat.

**Full caption**  
Conventional vegetable and seed oils often lean on three quiet regulars: **high omega-6** (common in soybean, corn, and similar blends), **oxidized lipids** when polyunsaturated fats meet a screaming-hot pan (or get reused), and **hexane residue** questions from some industrial refining paths. None of this is a panic poster. You’re not failing if the jug on your counter still says vegetable oil. You’re just learning what “neutral cooking oil” sometimes *means* in the fine print — so the next swap can be intentional. Quiet pans, clearer labels. Practical > perfect.
