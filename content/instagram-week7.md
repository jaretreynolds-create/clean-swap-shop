## Day 2 — Paraffin / fragrance / soot education

**Hook**  
“Cozy” on the jar doesn’t always mean simple in the wax.

**Full caption**  
Many conventional scented candles lean on three quiet regulars: **paraffin**, a wax refined from petroleum; **synthetic fragrance**, usually listed as one word with an undisclosed blend behind it; and **dyes** for that deep orange or plum color. Add **soot** — the black ring that shows up when wicks run long or flames flicker in a draft (any wax can do this; trim your wicks). None of this is a panic poster. You’re not failing if last night’s candle was a grocery-store jar. You’re just learning what “scented candle” sometimes *means* in the fine print — so the next one can be intentional. Golden light, shorter label. Practical > perfect.
