## Day 2 — Microplastics / knife groove education

**Hook**  
Every knife groove in a plastic board is plastic that went somewhere.

**Full caption**  
Most plastic cutting boards are **polyethylene** or **polypropylene**. Each chop leaves a tiny groove, and researchers have found that chopping on plastic boards can shed **microplastic** particles, some of which can end up in the food you're prepping. Deep grooves are also harder to clean, which is why worn plastic boards are meant to be replaced. That's the cycle: scar, stain, replace, repeat. This isn't a panic post. It's just what "dishwasher-safe board" can mean in practice, so the next one can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
