Hi friend,

**This week’s swap:** everyday bleach cleaner → **Branch Basics Concentrate**.

Bleach is familiar — and for good reason it’s still used for some heavy jobs. But for daily counters, sinks, and high-touch spots, that sharp “clean” smell is often chlorine chemistry, not proof the air is kinder to your lungs. Mixed wrong (think ammonia), bleach can create dangerous gas. Even used “right,” it can irritate airways and leave residue where coffee cups and kid snacks land.

**The clean swap:** Branch Basics Concentrate — a plant-based concentrate you dilute for all-purpose kitchen wipe-downs. Same spray-and-wipe ritual, gentler ingredient story, fewer single-use bottles crowding the cabinet.

**Toxin bite (keep it simple):** Chlorine bleach → respiratory irritation + VOC/residue concerns on everyday surfaces. Prefer a plant-based concentrate for daily cleaning; save harsh bleach for rare, well-ventilated deep cleans if you still need it.
