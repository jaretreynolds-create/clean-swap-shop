## Day 2 — Waste / liner education

**Hook**  
A holiday kitchen goes through liners faster than you think.

**Full caption**  
Three things worth a second look before you tear another sheet. **Parchment:** handy, usually single-use, and some greaseproof papers have historically raised PFAS questions. **Foil:** a one-use metal sheet that can tear or stick, especially on acidic or cheesy food. **Cooking spray:** extra residue and another can on the counter. This isn't a panic post. It's just noticing the pile by Friday, so the next tray can be a mat you rinse. Practical > perfect.
