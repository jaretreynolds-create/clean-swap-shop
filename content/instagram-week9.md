## Day 2 — Heat / black plastic education

**Hook**  
A melted spatula tip is plastic that went somewhere.

**Full caption**  
Most everyday cooking utensils are **black plastic** or **nylon** (polyamide), and they spend their lives in hot oil and simmering pots. A widely shared 2024 study found **flame retardants** in some black plastic kitchen utensils, likely from recycled electronics plastic. (The authors later corrected a math error in their exposure estimate, but the finding itself stood.) European food-safety agencies also advise keeping hot contact time short for nylon utensils, because they can release small plastic compounds at high heat. This isn't a panic post. It's just what "cheap and nonstick-safe" can mean in practice, so the next set can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
