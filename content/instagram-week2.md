## Day 2 — Sulfate education

**Hook**  
That big foamy lather isn’t always “cleaner.”

**Full caption**  
Sulfates (you’ll often see names like SLS or SLES on conventional labels) are foaming agents. They make shampoo feel “working” — lots of bubbles, that squeaky-clean rinse. For some scalps and hair types, that same action can over-strip natural oils, leave dryness or itch, and set you up for more product later just to feel soft again. Pair that with paraben preservatives and synthetic fragrance blends you can’t fully parse, and the everyday bottle gets louder than it needs to be. Education ≠ panic. You’re not failing if you still like foam. You’re just learning what the foam is *for* — so the next swap can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
