## Day 2 — Chlorine / PFAS / heavy metals education

**Hook**  
“Safe to drink” on the city report doesn’t always mean quiet in the glass.

**Full caption**  
Conventional tap often leans on three quiet regulars: **chlorine** (and sometimes chloramine) for disinfection, **PFAS** that show up in some systems as “forever chemicals,” and **heavy metals** questions when older pipes meet the faucet. None of this is a panic poster. You’re not failing if last night’s water came straight from the tap. You’re just learning what “municipal water” sometimes *means* in the fine print — so the next pour can be intentional. Quiet counters, clearer glasses. Practical > perfect.
