Most of us reach for baby lotion on autopilot — after baths, diaper changes, dry patches on tiny arms. None of this means you’re failing as a caregiver — it just means the default baby aisle isn’t always the gentlest option for new skin.

**The clean swap:** Earth Mama Organics — a clean, organic-leaning lotion path that aims for the same soft ritual (warm hands, gentle rub-in) with a shorter, more readable ingredient story. Think cream nurseries, linen swaddles, and terracotta night-lights — not a full baby-product overhaul. You’re not tossing every bottle tonight. Finish what you have, then swap the next one you buy.

**Toxin bite (keep it simple):** Parabens show up as preservatives in many formulas; mineral oil is a common petroleum-derived filler that can feel occlusive without adding much plant-based care; PEGs help texture but aren’t the cleanest story for skin you’re still learning to protect. Prefer a clean organic lotion when you’re ready for the next bottle.
