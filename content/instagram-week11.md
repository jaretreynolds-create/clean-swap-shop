## Day 2 — Fragrance / dye education

**Hook**  
Why is your dish soap neon blue?

**Full caption**  
Two things worth a second look on a dish soap label. **"Fragrance":** U.S. labeling rules let brands list a whole scent blend as one word, so you can't see what's in it. **Dyes:** they make the bottle pop on the shelf and do nothing for your plates. Add hands in hot water several times a day during the holidays, and simpler starts to sound good. This isn't a panic post. It's just reading the label, so the next bottle can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
