### ☐ 4. Scented trash bags / “odor control” liners

| | |
|---|---|
| **Toxic clue** | Bags that smell like candy or “fresh linen” before anything hits the bin; fragrance masking garbage instead of emptying it. Synthetic scent = more VOCs in a closed cabinet. |
| **Clean swap** | Switch to **unscented** liners. Empty more often. Optional: a drop of essential oil on a cotton pad *outside* the bag, or a diffuser in the room — not perfume baked into plastic. |
| **Shop (fresh air path)** | [Shop essential oil diffuser →](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=essential+oil+diffuser&tag=cleanswapsh0a-20) · [Guide: Air freshener vs diffuser](https://www.cleanswapshop.com/pages/guides/air-freshener-vs-diffuser.html) |

### ☐ 5. Sponges & scrubbers (notes, not panic)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Toxic clue** | Permanently damp cellulose that smells like a swamp; antibacterial “kill germs” coatings you don’t need for dishes; microplastic scrubbers shedding into the drain. |
| **Clean swap** | Rotate sponges often; wring dry; microwave damp sponge briefly or run through dishwasher if the brand allows. Prefer **compostable cellulose**, **wood-pulp scrubbers**, or washable Swedish-style cloths. Replace on a schedule — smell is your friend. |
| **Shop note** | No single must-buy — keep it simple and biodegradable when you restock. Browse kitchen ideas: [All Swaps](https://www.cleanswapshop.com/pages/swaps.html) |

---

### ☐ 6. Cleaning gloves (quick note)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Toxic clue** | Soft PVC/vinyl gloves can contain phthalates; strong “new plastic” smell; powder that irritates skin. |
| **Clean swap** | Choose **nitrile** or natural-rubber options when you can; skip heavily fragranced “lotion” gloves. Wash reusable gloves and hang to dry. Bare hands + Branch Basics is fine for light jobs. |
| **Shop note** | Optional restock — prioritize fit and non-PVC when you replace them. |
