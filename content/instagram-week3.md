## Day 2 — Parabens / mineral oil / PEGs education

**Hook**  
“Gentle” on the front doesn’t always mean quiet on the back.

**Full caption**  
Conventional baby lotions often lean on three quiet regulars: **parabens** (preservatives you’ll see as methylparaben, propylparaben, and friends), **mineral oil** (a petroleum-derived filler that can feel slick without adding plant-based nourishment), and **PEGs** (petroleum-based thickeners/emulsifiers that help that silky squeeze). None of this is a panic poster. You’re not failing if the bottle on your shelf still says Johnson’s — or any drugstore twin. You’re just learning what “gentle” sometimes *means* in the fine print — so the next swap can be intentional. Soft skin, clearer labels. Practical > perfect.
