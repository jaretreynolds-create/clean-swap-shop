## Day 2 — Petrolatum / flavor education

**Hook**  
If it's on your lips, it's in your mouth.

**Full caption**  
Two things worth a second look on a chapstick label. **Petrolatum:** a mineral-oil barrier that seals, but isn't a plant oil. **"Flavor" / "fragrance":** U.S. labeling rules let brands list a whole blend as one word, so you can't see what's in it. Add five reapplications a day in dry November air, and a four-ingredient tube starts to sound good. This isn't a panic post. It's just reading the label, so the next tube can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
