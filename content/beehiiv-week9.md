**The clean swap:** a teak utensil set. Teak is dense and naturally water-resistant, it won't melt if it rests on the pan edge, and it's gentle on nonstick. The Zulay set covers the whole crock: spatula, turner, slotted turner, ladle, skimmer, serving spoon, pasta server, and a salad spoon and fork. Warm brown wood grain next to the stove, and it comes in a sliding gift box (holiday list, anyone?). Care is simple: hand wash, dry fully, no dishwasher. You don't have to empty the crock tonight. **Toxin bite (keep it simple):** A widely shared 2024 study found flame retardants in some black plastic kitchen utensils, likely from recycled electronics plastic (the authors later corrected a math error in their exposure estimate, but the finding itself stood). And European food-safety agencies advise keeping hot contact time short for nylon utensils. Wood sidesteps both.
