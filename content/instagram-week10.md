## Day 2 — Heat / plastic education

**Hook**  
Plastic + boiling water is the pairing worth a second look.

**Full caption**  
Heat is what makes plastic most likely to shed. A 2020 study in **Nature Food** found that polypropylene baby bottles released **microplastic particles** when filled with hot water, and hotter water meant more particles. Kettles weren't the subject, but the setup is the same: very hot water against plastic, every day. Add the "new kettle" plastic taste so many reviews mention, plus scale you can't see in an opaque kettle, and glass starts to look pretty good. This isn't a panic post. It's just what "cheap and fast" can mean, so the next kettle can be intentional. Practical > perfect.
