## Day 2 — Quats / fragrance / film education

**Hook**  
“Soft and fresh” on the box doesn’t always mean quiet on your clothes.

**Full caption**  
Conventional dryer sheets often lean on three quiet regulars: **quats** (quaternary ammonium compounds) for softening and static control, **synthetic fragrance** that lingers on skin-contact fabric, and a **softener film** that can leave towels less absorbent than they should be. Add the single-use sheet in the trash every load. None of this is a panic poster. You’re not failing if last night’s towels came out of a scented sheet cycle. You’re just learning what “fabric softener sheet” sometimes *means* in the fine print — so the next load can be intentional. Quiet laundry rooms, clearer clothes. Practical > perfect.
